Built for Limitless
Creativity

Our mission was clear: not just to refine a sequencer, but to build an instrument you can truly play. An extension of your creativity, not a barrier to it. The OXI ONE MKII is a creative powerhouse built to keep you in the flow — whether you’re composing intricate melodies, sculpting grooves, or performing live.

Versatile Sequencing for Every Style

Multitrack
8 tracks with totally independent parameters: start-end points, length, speed, etc. Configurable MIDI outputs and scale note filtering. Did you miss having more tracks? Now you can unlock up to 32 tracks in the OXI ONE.
Chord
An Innovative chord function generator for easy creation of chord progressions, with a wide palette of chord types and voicings that complement each other. Featuring Chromatic Mode Chords and custom scales for even more harmonic flexibility.
Poly
Total freedom with up to 7 voices and plenty of parameters per step. Forget your DAW piano roll for a moment and enjoy this new creative editing experience with unique features like per note chance or logic conditions.
Mono
Take full control of a monophonic track, with clear, detailed visualization. OXI ONE MKII offers intuitive editing and direct access. Enhanced with accumulators, providing even more options for intricate melody making.
Stochastic
Craft the perfect recipe for evolving melodies. With OXI ONE MKII’S stochastic mode, randomness becomes your creative partner - offering infinite variations while keeping full control over rhythm and pitch.
Matriceal
Break the rules of traditional sequencing. Decouple steps from velocity, octaves, notes, and more. Four independent lanes put you in full control—flexible, unpredictable, and built for experimentation.

Dive into Harmony
with Chord Mode

Our groundbreaking CHORD MODE allows you to easily craft dynamic chord progressions, offering a wide range of chord types, voicings, spread, and bass options—all organized within intuitive chord menus. With the built-in chord arpeggiator, you can seamlessly arpeggiate your chords and create rich, evolving musical landscapes.

150+ Chord
Variations

Thanks to the HARMONIZER, you can synchronize other instruments to follow your chord progressions, ensuring perfect harmonic coherence across your setup.

Dynamic Chord Modulation

Modulate your chord track using any of the available modulation sources (LFO, MOD lanes, or External CV). Any modulation you apply will be translated to the accompanying instruments, ensuring that your creation is always evolving.
chord mode
Shape Your Sound

Modulation Lanes

Modulation Lanes empower you to infuse dynamic movement into your compositions directly from the OXI One MKII. Whether you’re drawing automation curves on the grid, recording real-time encoder movements, or manually adjusting parameters, these lanes offer unparalleled flexibility.
1
Independent Length and Speed
Each modulation lane operates with its own length and speed settings, allowing for complex, evolving modulations.
2
Versatile Routing
Modulation lanes can be assigned to both internal parameters and external devices, providing extensive control over your setup.
3
CV Output Integration
Any modulation lane can be routed to any CV output, enabling seamless integration with modular systems.
