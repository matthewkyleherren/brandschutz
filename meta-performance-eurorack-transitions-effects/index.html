Effect Scenes Breakdown

Meta FX Scenes were crafted and tested to deliver premium level FX.
With simplicity, but also allowing to tweak enough to fit your taste and technical needs.

SPACE

Send your mix into the outer realms with this reverb effect.
Choose from two types of reverbs, a classic long tail reverb with tone and filter control or an experimental reverb from shimmery to deep/black-hole textures.

RISER

One of most effective ways to create tension for drops and transitions. The OXI Meta riser features two modes both with a high-pass filter & reverb. Mode 1 adds noise and mode 2 adds a Shepard tone-style effect.

DJ FILTER

A DJ style filter is must have for any live performer. OXI Meta provides you with a high-pass and low-pass filter for the ultimate in transitions.

LOOP

Loop small sections of audio in forward or reverse order to create head-nodding breaks and rhythms. 

DELAY

A high-pass or low-pass clockable delay algorithm with a splash of reverb wash to help glue this classic effect into the mix.

GATER-ROLLER

A unique dual gater effect for exciting audio. Turn the knob right for a gater with division control and built-in high-pass filter, or go left with a snare roll build up with reverb and built-in high-pass filter.
